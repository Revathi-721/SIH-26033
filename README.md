## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose developing a smart digital agricultural marketplace that connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers through a single online platform.

The platform eliminates unnecessary intermediaries and enables farmers to sell their products directly at fair prices.

Key features:
- Direct Marketplace: Connect farmers and FPOs directly with consumers, supermarkets, retailers, and bulk buyers.
- Smart Price Management: Display market prices and help farmers determine fair selling prices.
- Logistics Integration: Connect farmers with transport providers for efficient product delivery.
- AI Demand Forecasting: Predict future demand using historical sales, seasonal trends, and market data.
- AI Route Optimization: Suggest efficient delivery routes to reduce transportation costs and delivery time.
- Real-Time Tracking: Allow buyers and farmers to track orders and deliveries.

## TecWe will develop the platform using modern web technologies, database management, AI, and cloud services.

MySQL / MongoDB:	Store user, product and order details

Google Maps: API	Route optimization and location tracking
REST APIs:	Communication between frontend and backend
Cloud :Hosting	Secure data storage and platform deploymenthnical Approach
Working Process:
1. Farmers register and upload their available products, quantities, and prices.
2. Consumers and bulk buyers browse products and place orders.
3. The platform connects orders with suitable farmers.
4. AI predicts demand and helps farmers plan production and inventory.
5. Logistics providers receive delivery requests.
6. Route optimization identifies efficient delivery routes.
7. Buyers receive their products directly from farmers.

## Feasibility and Viability
The proposed solution is technically, economically, and operationally feasible.

Technical Feasibility
- Can be developed using affordable, open-source technologies.
- AI models can be trained using historical agricultural market data.
- GPS and mapping services can support delivery management.
Economic Feasibility
- Reduces unnecessary intermediary commissions.
- Minimizes transportation expenses through optimized routes.
- Offers an affordable platform for small-scale farmers and FPOs.
Operational Feasibility
- Simple mobile-friendly interface for farmers and buyers.
- Supports regional languages to improve accessibility.
- Can initially be implemented in selected villages and expanded gradually.
Business Viability
- Revenue can be generated through small transaction fees, logistics partnerships, and subscription plans for bulk buyers.
- Partnerships with FPOs, agricultural cooperatives, and transport providers can support long-term growth.
